# Jump to a section

Long pages in the demo app have a navigation menu at the top. Use it to jump straight
to the section you need instead of scrolling.

## Before you begin

Open the demo app home page at `http://localhost:8092`.

## Jump to the lists

1. In the navigation menu, click **Lists**.

   The page scrolls to the **Lists** section. It contains three kinds of list:
   **Unordered List**, **Ordered List**, and **Definition List**.

2. Check the ordered list. Its last entry is **Third item**.

## Jump to the tables

3. Scroll back to the top and click **Tables** in the navigation menu.

   The page scrolls to the **Simple Data Table**. You should see the record for
   **Bob Johnson**.
