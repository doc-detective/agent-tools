# Preview notification messages

The message preview panel lets you check how notifications look before you send them
to your team. Each button adds a sample message to the panel.

## Add sample messages

1. Go to `http://localhost:8092/click-shorthand.html`.

2. Click **Reveal by text**.

   The message **Text click landed** appears below the buttons.

3. Click **Reveal by object**.

   A second message, **Object click landed**, appears under the first one. Earlier
   messages stay in the panel until you reload the page.

> **Tip:** Reload the page to clear the panel and start over.
