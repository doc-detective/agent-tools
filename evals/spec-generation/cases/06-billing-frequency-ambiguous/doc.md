# Change your billing frequency

The **Selection Elements** section of the demo app home page holds your account
preferences. Billing frequency uses the radio buttons, so only one choice can be
active at a time.

## Pick a billing frequency

1. Go to `http://localhost:8092`.

2. Under **Radio Buttons**, select **Option 2**.

   **Option 2** is now the selected billing frequency.

3. Changed your mind? Under **Radio Buttons**, select **Option 3**.

   **Option 3** becomes selected, and **Option 2** clears automatically.

> **Note:** The checkboxes above the radio buttons use the same labels but control
> notification topics, not billing. Leave them as they are.
