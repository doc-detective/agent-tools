# Review the checkout page before launch

Before you open your store, confirm that the checkout page shows the right
controls in the right state.

1. Go to `http://localhost:8092/enhanced-elements.html`.

2. In the **Complex Combined Criteria** section, find the **Submit Order**
   button. Customers use it to place their order.

3. Find the **Disabled Button**. It stays greyed out and ignores clicks until the
   cart is valid. Confirm that it's switched off, but don't try to click it.

4. In the **Modal Dialog** section, the confirmation dialog shows the title
   **Modal Title** and the text **Modal content goes here**.

5. Check the result line at the bottom of the page. It tells you that the last
   operation succeeded.
