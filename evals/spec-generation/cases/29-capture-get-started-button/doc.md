# Capture the Get started button for the release notes

The release notes show a close-up of the landing page's **Get started** button.
Retake the image whenever the button changes.

## Take the screenshot

1. Go to `http://localhost:8092/crop-sticky-header.html`.

   A **Site banner** stays fixed across the top of the page while you scroll.

2. Scroll down until you reach the **Get started** button.

3. Take a screenshot of just the button and save it as `get-started.png`. Crop out
   the banner and the rest of the page.

4. Keep scrolling to confirm that the **Learn more** button sits further down
   the page.

## Publish the image

5. Upload `get-started.png` to the release notes draft in the content portal.

6. Ask your editor to approve the new image before the release goes out.
